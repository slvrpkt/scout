Background timers

Inside Scout; they only run while Scout is running.

  • Agent runs: at your run times (e.g. 9:00 AM, 1:00 PM), checked every minute; a missed time runs when Scout is back
  • Scoring: about 5 minutes after a search; a batch is checked every 2 minutes
  • Cleanup: every 15 minutes, old task data and archived agent jobs past their time (90 days by default)
  • Nightly backup: 3:00 AM
  • Base resume watch: refits it after your profile changes
  • Inbox check: Gmail replies, if connected
  • Update check: GitHub, daily, off by default

Job runner

Longer work in the background, step by step, with progress on screen.

  • Agent search: every saved search, page by page; skips jobs already seen
  • Set aside first: titles to skip, words, distance, minimum score, for free
  • Scoring: what's left, one by one or as a batch
  • Drafts: tailored resume and cover letter for the best scores
  • ATS check: grades a resume and improves it toward an A
  • Page fit: the base resume at exactly two or three pages
  • Files: PDFs and Word files to download

Database POSTGRESQL

  • Your profile and base resume
  • Applications, their documents and history
  • The agent's searches and the jobs it found
  • Saved for later and the Archive
  • Settings, run times, limits and costs
  • API keys, encrypted with your secret

Files and rendering

  • PDFs: printed by headless Chromium, so they match the preview
  • Word files: built as .docx, pages checked
  • Nightly backups: kept for a set number of days
  • Exports: everything, as one file, whenever you ask
The internet (your keys)

AI provider COSTS PER USE

Claude Haiku (default), or OpenAI, Mistral, or any OpenAI-compatible service.

Used for: fit scores, tailored resumes, cover letters, ATS fixes, reading your resume.

Gets the parts of your profile and the posting each task needs. Scoring can go as a batch at half price. Your spending limit stops requests once it's reached.

Or a local model (Ollama, LM Studio): free, and nothing leaves your computer.

Job source COSTS PER SEARCH

JSearch on RapidAPI (default): Google for Jobs, which covers LinkedIn, Indeed, Glassdoor and company career sites. Paid per request; each page of results is one.

Or Adzuna, FreeHire, Bundesagentur für Arbeit.

Gets only the search: words, place, filters. Never your profile.

Other services OPTIONAL

  • TypeSafe Jev: a scoring model, instead of the AI provider
  • GitHub: the update check, off until you switch it on
  • Employer and job board pages: Scout opens a posting's own page to read the rest of its text

Scout sends no telemetry or usage data.
