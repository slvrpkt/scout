Scout Architecture: A Bird's-Eye View

Everything runs on your own computer. Only the LLM provider and the job source are reached over the internet, with your own keys; the rest is optional.

Your Computer (localhost)
The Internet (Your Own Keys)

You, in Your Browser

Signed in with your password. Every screen in one place:

  • Dashboard
  • Agentic and Manual job search
  • Saved for later
  • Job application log and Archive
  • Profile, Base resume, Settings

Claude OPTIONAL

Claude Desktop or claude.ai can use Scout's connector (MCP) to read postings and help draft.

Google OPTIONAL

Gmail for sending and replies, Calendar for interviews, and Drive: a folder per job you applied to, with the resume and cover letter you sent.

Scout Web App NEXT.JS · NODE

Draws every screen and handles every click. Checks your sign-in on each request, keeps API keys encrypted (never sent to the browser), applies your spending limits, and flags jobs you've already applied to.

Job Agent THE CORE

  • Runs every saved search at your times
  • Asks the job source for new postings, page by page
  • Skips jobs it has already seen
  • Sets aside what your rules rule out, for free
  • Asks the LLM to score each job against your profile (batch, half price)
  • Ranks the best; 80+ get drafts

Background Timers

  • Agent runs at your times, checked every minute
  • Scoring about 5 minutes after a search
  • Cleanup every 15 minutes, incl. the Archive
  • Nightly backup at 3:00 AM
  • Inbox and update checks, if on

Job Runner

  • Drafts a tailored resume and cover letter (LLM)
  • Grades it against ATS criteria and fixes it toward an A (LLM)
  • Fits the base resume to 2 or 3 pages (LLM)
  • Builds PDF and Word files

Database POSTGRESQL

  • Profile and base resume
  • Applications, documents, history
  • Agent searches and jobs found
  • Saved for later and the Archive
  • Settings, limits, costs; keys encrypted

Files

  • PDFs, printed like the preview
  • Word files, pages checked
  • Nightly backups, kept for a set time
  • Exports of everything

Job Source COSTS PER SEARCH

JSearch on RapidAPI by default: Google for Jobs, covering LinkedIn, Indeed, Glassdoor and company career sites. Or Adzuna, FreeHire, Bundesagentur. Gets only the search, never your profile.

LLM Provider COSTS PER USE

Claude Haiku by default; or OpenAI, Mistral, any OpenAI-compatible service.

For the agent: scores each job's fit. For the runner: writes resumes and cover letters, grades them against ATS criteria, reads your resume. Gets only what each task needs; your spending limit stops it.

Or a local model (Ollama, LM Studio): free, nothing leaves your computer.

Other Services OPTIONAL

  • Employer and job board pages, to read a posting's full text
  • TypeSafe Jev, a scoring model instead of the LLM
  • GitHub update check, off until switched on

Scout sends no telemetry or usage data.
