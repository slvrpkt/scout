## Changes from Dossier

Scout has been extensively modified since 2026, including:

- An agentic job search: saved searches with alternative titles and up to ten locations, run on a schedule, with skip rules, filters and daily budgets.
- Fit scoring by the user's own rubric (AI fit, work style, pay, distance from a US ZIP code and a freshness bonus), with a breakdown of each score.
- A grade for tailored resumes against common US applicant-tracking (ATS) criteria, with automatic rewrites toward an A.
- Tailored resumes written from the full job description and the full profile, with outcome-driven writing rules.
- Exact page limits for resumes in both PDF and Word: older jobs condensed first, points rewritten rather than cut, and last-resort trims for skills lines, irrelevant certifications and tools.
- A base resume at Full, two and three pages; education on one line with an optional graduation year.
- Manual job search filters, a job application log, an archive, duplicate warnings and many interface changes.
- Scout's own name, links and update check; no telemetry.

The full history of changes is in this repository's commits.
